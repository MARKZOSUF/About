## 👨‍💻 About The Operator
I am a recent PCM graduate and a tech enthusiast deeply invested in the intersection of hardware engineering and digital security. I don't just use technology—I dismantle, modify, and build it from the ground up. Whether it's configuring network interfaces on Kali Linux or soldering custom parts for a DIY build, I am always exploring how systems function at their core.

- 🔭 **Current Project:** Building and optimizing a rugged DIY Cyber-Deck from salvaged hardware.
- 🌱 **Currently Learning:** Advanced network security, Bash scripting, and Python automation.
- ⚡ **Fun Fact:** I love optimizing low-spec PCs and repurposing old electronics.
